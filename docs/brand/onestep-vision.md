# Onestep Vision Brand Guide

## Name

- Product name: **Onestep Vision**
- Parent brand: **ONESTEP**
- Preferred lockup text: **Onestep Vision** with supporting copy **by ONESTEP** when parent context is needed.
- Short descriptor: **Image intelligence workspace**

Do not refer to the product as a diagnostic system or autonomous medical decision-maker.

## Logo

The app uses concept 1 from the brand assets: a segmented circular vision aperture with speed lines and a forward arrow. The mark communicates image focus, workflow motion, and the ONESTEP idea of progress.

Brand assets:

- `frontend/public/brand/logo_transparent.png` for UI chrome, favicon metadata, and placements on controlled backgrounds.
- `frontend/public/brand/logo_dark.png` for dark brand tiles, presentations, and previews where a dark framed background is needed.
- `frontend/public/brand/logo_light.png` for white or light-background documentation and presentation use.

Usage guidance:

- Use `logo_transparent.png` in the app shell so the mark can sit inside the Onestep Vision brand tile.
- Use the dark or light PNG variants as complete logo tiles when the surrounding surface should not control the mark background.
- Keep generous padding around the mark.
- Do not stretch, crop, recolor randomly, or place the mark on busy imagery.
- Do not copy unrelated ONESTEP public logo assets into the app.

## Color Tokens

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#111B2B` | Soft dark sidebar, strong text, logo base |
| Deep navy | `#182536` | Secondary dark surfaces and dark gradients |
| Vision teal | `#14B8A6` | Primary product accent, vision/lens motif |
| Step green | `#8BCF5A` | Progress, selected state, forward motion |
| Signal coral | `#F9735B` | Warning/accent emphasis |
| Mist surface | `#F6F8FB` | Page background and quiet surfaces |
| Line | `#D7DDE5` | Borders and separators |

The UI green should feel like a calm product accent, not a neon highlight. Reserve it for active navigation rails, progress cues, selected states, and small brand moments. Pair it with Vision teal for image-intelligence context and with the softened navy sidebar for maximum visibility.

## Voice

Use concise, confident product language:

- Good: “Create an image intelligence workspace.”
- Good: “Compare model behavior across datasets.”
- Good: “Prepare local datasets without rewriting originals.”
- Avoid: “Automatically diagnose disease.”
- Avoid: “Clinically approved decision engine.”

## UI Tone

Onestep Vision should feel like focused product software: dense, calm, precise, and trustworthy. Use cards for repeated entities, compact chips for metadata, and restrained color accents for task states.
