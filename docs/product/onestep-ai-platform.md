# Onestep AI Platform Product Vision

## Product Promise

Onestep AI Platform is one workspace for image intelligence: a local studio where teams can turn image datasets into usable model experiments through labeling, preparation, training, testing, and inspection.

The product is built for research and engineering workflows. It helps teams move from raw images to measurable model behavior without implying autonomous clinical diagnosis or final decision-making.

## Audience

- Product and engineering teams building image AI workflows.
- Researchers comparing datasets, annotations, models, and evaluation runs.
- Applied ML teams that need local control over assets, experiments, and generated artifacts.

## Workflow Pillars

- **Organize:** Create projects and datasets that keep image work scoped and understandable.
- **Prepare:** Upload, label, annotate, split, preprocess, and version datasets without rewriting originals.
- **Train:** Run task-compatible training jobs from prepared datasets.
- **Test:** Compare model behavior with task-aware metrics and per-image inspection.
- **Inspect:** Keep model outputs, history, and dataset health visible for repeated iteration.

## UX Principles

- **Operational first:** The first screen should be useful workspace UI, not a marketing page.
- **Dense but calm:** Prioritize scanning, comparison, and repeated action over decorative layouts.
- **Clear provenance:** Make project, dataset, split, model, and run context visible.
- **Local trust:** Keep uploads, predictions, generated versions, and runtime artifacts under local ignored storage.
- **No clinical claims:** Use research and engineering language only.

## Positioning Line

One workspace for image intelligence.

## Brand Asset Source
